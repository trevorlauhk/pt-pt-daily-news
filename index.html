English

Yesterday, Maria went to the post office to send a package to her grandmother. She took a big box with sweets, a scarf, and a letter. Before leaving home, she weighed the box on the kitchen scale. It weighed two and a half kilos, so she thought: "It’s going to be expensive!" At the post office, the line was long, but Maria didn’t get upset. She heard a lady talking to the clerk about the prices. Then, it was her turn. The clerk weighed the box again and said: "That’ll be three euros and fifty cents." Maria paid and got a receipt. Before leaving, she asked: "How long will it take to arrive?" The clerk answered: "It’ll take three or four days." Maria smiled and said: "Great! Grandma will be very happy." On her way home, she bought a coffee and thought about her grandmother opening the box. How nice!

Liberdade de Expressão ou Liberdade para Mentir? O Dilema das Redes Sociais

English

In recent years, social media has become the main stage for public debate. While these platforms have democratized the voices of millions, allowing ideas to circulate without traditional filters, they have also opened the door to a worrying phenomenon: disinformation. Although freedom of expression is a fundamental pillar of democratic societies, it is undeniable that its unlimited exercise on social media can have serious consequences, since fake news spreads faster than verified facts. However, the solution to this problem is not simple. Some argue that digital platforms should moderate content more rigorously so that the truth prevails. But who decides what is true? And, more importantly, to what extent is it legitimate to restrict freedom of expression, even when it is used to spread lies? It is crucial that a balance be found, but achieving it requires reflection and, above all, collective responsibility. Therefore, perhaps the answer lies not just in laws or algorithms, but in education. As long as people develop critical thinking, they will be better prepared to distinguish facts from fiction. After all, an informed society is the best defense against manipulation, and this will only be possible if each of us takes on the commitment to verify before sharing. Let us not forget: freedom of expression is a right, but also a responsibility.
