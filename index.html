English

Yesterday, Rita left home to buy a birthday cake. She wanted to go to her grandmother’s bakery, but she didn’t know the way. So, she decided to ask for directions on the street. First, she saw an old man with a dog. Rita asked, "Excuse me, do you know where Grandma’s bakery is?" The man smiled and said, "Yes, it’s near the square." Rita thanked him, but after walking for two minutes, she realized she was lost. Then, she met a woman with shopping bags. The woman replied, "Oh, it’s easy! Go straight until the traffic light." Rita followed the instructions, but she didn’t see any bank. Finally, a boy on a bicycle passed by her. The boy laughed: "It’s right here! Come with me!" In the end, Rita arrived at the bakery and bought the cake. She learned that sometimes, it’s better to ask for help more than once!

O 25 de Abril: Uma Revolução que Cheirou a Liberdade e a Jasmim

English

Almost fifty years ago, Portugal woke up to a new dawn. Although many do not remember or did not live through that day, April 25, 1974, remains a landmark that changed the course of the country’s history. It was not just a military revolution; above all, it was a collective cry for freedom, democracy, and dignity—values that many of us today take for granted. However, it wasn’t always like this. For nearly half a century, Portugal lived under the yoke of a dictatorship that controlled every aspect of daily life. People could not freely express their opinions, books were censored, and those who dared to challenge the regime risked imprisonment or exile. Yet, there was something the regime could not control: hope. Even in the darkest moments, there were those who believed that one day Portugal would breathe freedom again. Then, in the early hours of that historic day, the military members of the Armed Forces Movement (MFA) advanced so that the country could finally choose its own destiny. There was no significant resistance, since the population, tired of decades of oppression, took to the streets en masse to support the soldiers. The images of red carnations tucked into the rifles of the military became the symbol of a peaceful revolution, something rare in world history. Today, when we look back, it is important that we do not forget the meaning of that day. The freedom we enjoy today was not handed to us on a silver platter; it was won with courage and determination. Therefore, it is our duty to honor those who fought so that we could live in a fairer and freer country. Despite some challenges we still face, April 25th taught us that change is possible, as long as there is unity and collective will. May this date always serve as a reminder: freedom is a right, but also a responsibility that we must cultivate every day.
