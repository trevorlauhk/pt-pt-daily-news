Yesterday, Ana went to the City Hall because she needed a new document. She woke up early and had breakfast quickly. Then, she put on nice clothes and left home. The bus arrived at eight-thirty, and she sat by the window. When she arrived at the City Hall, she saw many people in line. Ana waited half an hour, but it wasn’t bad because she read a book on her phone. Then, she spoke to the clerk and handed in the papers. The clerk said, "Come back in three days to pick up the document." Ana left happy and went for a coffee. Before going home, she stopped by the supermarket and bought bread and milk. At night, she told her husband: "Tomorrow I’ll go back to the City Hall to pick up the document." Her husband laughed and said, "Then I’ll go with you to help carry the groceries!"
