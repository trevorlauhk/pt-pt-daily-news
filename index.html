Yesterday, Ana and Pedro went to the zoo. They woke up early because they wanted to see all the animals. Before leaving, their mom said, "Take water and a hat!" On the bus, Pedro said, "I like lions the most." Ana replied, "I prefer the monkeys." When they arrived, they saw the elephants first. Ana laughed because a giraffe licked her hand. At lunchtime, they ate sandwiches and drank juice. Pedro also bought an ice cream. Before leaving, they saw the penguins. Ana took lots of photos. On the way home, they were tired but happy. "Tomorrow we’ll go to the park," said Pedro. Ana agreed: "Yes, but today was the best day!"

English

Some say happiness has no address, yet the truth is that where we live deeply shapes our daily lives. While some cannot imagine life without the constant hustle and bustle of cities, others only feel truly at peace when surrounded by the silence of the countryside. But after all, what is the ideal setting for a fulfilling life? Living in the city offers undeniable advantages: immediate access to services, culture, jobs, and a diversity of people that, though it may make the environment more impersonal, enriches our worldview. However, the fast pace, noise, and pollution are factors that, in the long run, can wear down even the most resilient spirits. That is why many dream of trading asphalt for greenery, so they can breathe fresh air and slow down. On the other hand, country life brings a tranquility that, for some, is synonymous with monotony. Distances are greater, services scarcer, and at times, the feeling of isolation can be overwhelming. Still, those who live in rural areas often highlight the closeness to nature, the tighter-knit community, and the fact that time seems to flow in a more human way. Since the routine is not dictated by the clock but by the cycle of the sun, some argue that this is the true key to a balanced life. Therefore, the choice between city and countryside is not simple, nor should it be made lightly. What matters is that each person finds their own rhythm, whether fast-paced or serene. After all, what truly counts is that we feel at home, wherever our heart beats strongest.
