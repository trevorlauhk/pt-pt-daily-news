English

In recent years, the internet has become the main stage for public debate. However, this borderless freedom has brought with it a complex dilemma: how to balance the right to freedom of expression with the need to combat disinformation? Although many argue that everyone should have a voice, it is undeniable that some opinions spread dangerous lies, capable of influencing elections, spreading panic, or even putting lives at risk. Since social media has become the primary source of information for millions of people, it is crucial that mechanisms exist to prevent the dissemination of falsehoods. Yet, who decides what is true or false? Governments and digital platforms have the power to censor content, but does this not open the door to abuses? Consequently, many wonder if we are not moving toward a scenario where freedom of expression is merely an illusion. In order for democracy not to be eroded by disinformation, a middle ground must be found. Perhaps the solution lies in greater digital literacy, where citizens are taught to verify sources and think critically. After all, freedom of expression should not be a shield for those who spread hatred or falsehoods, but rather a right that comes with responsibility. Therefore, the challenge is not simple, but it is urgent: how to ensure that everyone’s voice is heard without the truth getting lost in the digital noise?
