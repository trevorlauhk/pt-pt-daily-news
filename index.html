English

Grandma Maria didn't feel well yesterday. She had a headache and a fever. This morning, she said to her grandson João: "I'm going to the pharmacy to buy medicine." João replied: "Of course, Grandma! I'll get my wallet right away." At the pharmacy, Grandma spoke to the pharmacist: "Good morning! I need medicine for a headache and fever." The pharmacist asked: "Do you have a prescription?" Grandma Maria answered: "No, I don’t. It’s just a mild pain." The pharmacist explained: "Then, I can give you paracetamol. Take one tablet every eight hours." Grandma thanked her and asked: "How much does it cost?" João paid with Grandma’s wallet and put the medicine in her bag. Then, he said: "Grandma, let’s go home. You need to rest." On the way home, João also bought a bottle of water. "Drink water with the medicine, Grandma," he said. Grandma laughed: "Yes, Doctor João!"

English

In recent years, climate change has been one of the most debated topics worldwide. Although many governments and organizations have already taken steps to reduce their ecological footprint, the truth is that the results are still not enough. Rising temperatures, sea-level rise, and the increasing frequency of extreme events like droughts and floods are clear signs that the planet is changing—and not for the better. However, we cannot give in to despair. There is still hope, as long as each of us does our part. Small actions, such as reducing plastic consumption, choosing public transport, or even planting a tree, may seem insignificant, but when multiplied by millions of people, they make a difference. It is crucial that citizens demand more ambitious policies from their leaders so that sustainability goals are met. Yet, the challenge is not just individual. Companies, therefore, play a crucial role in this process. Many have already adopted greener practices, such as using renewable energy or reducing CO₂ emissions. Since the private sector is responsible for a large part of global pollution, it is essential that it continues to innovate and invest in sustainable solutions. Despite the long road ahead, we cannot give up. The future of our planet depends on the decisions we make today. Therefore, it is time to act—before it is too late.
