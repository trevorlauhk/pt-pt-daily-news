English

Yesterday, Ana went to the city center. She wanted to buy a custard tart, but she didn’t know where the famous bakery was. So, she decided to ask for directions. First, she saw a lady at the bus stop. Ana said, "Excuse me, do you know where the bakery 'Doce Sonho' is?" The lady smiled and replied, "Of course! Go straight ahead and turn onto the second street on the right." Ana thanked her and started walking. But after five minutes, she didn’t see any bakery. So, she stopped a young man who was walking his dog. The young man thought for a moment and said, "Ah, yes! The lady was mistaken." Ana laughed and followed the new directions. She bought two custard tarts and went home very happy. On the way, she thought, "Next time, I’ll ask two people to be sure!"

O Planeta Pede Socorro: Será que Ainda Vamos a Tempo?

🐶 Oreo Sir 小 Tips
汪汪！Oreo Sir 來啦！🍪 親愛的港漂同學們，今天要跟大家分享一個超級重要的「餅乾秘訣」：當你用 *a fim de que* 或 *desde que* 時，記得後面要加 *conjuntivo*（虛擬式）哦！比如「A fim de que *façamos* a diferença...」，這樣句子就會像我啃餅乾一樣順滑！🍪 別怕用複雜連接詞，它們能讓你的文章更有深度，就像我尾巴上的毛一樣蓬鬆有型！加油，你們都是未來的葡語大師！汪～

English

In recent years, climate change has dominated the headlines, but are we truly taking action to combat it? Despite many governments and companies promising to reduce carbon emissions, concrete results are still scarce. However, we cannot ignore that every small action counts, since the planet's future depends on the decisions we make today. It is undeniable that sustainability has become a buzzword in modern vocabulary, yet its practical application remains a challenge. For example, although many of us know we should recycle or avoid single-use plastics, few are willing to radically change their habits. Therefore, it is essential that awareness campaigns become more effective so that people understand the urgency of the situation. Moreover, the younger generations have shown greater environmental awareness, which gives us hope. As long as young people start demanding real change, perhaps we can pressure world leaders to take more ambitious measures. After all, it is not enough to talk about sustainability; we must act before it is too late. The planet does not need heroes, but responsible citizens willing to make a difference.
