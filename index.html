English

Yesterday, Pedro went to the City Hall for the first time. He needed an important document for work. When he arrived, he saw a long line at the entrance. "Oh no!" he thought. "I’ll be here all day." But then, a kind clerk said, "Don’t worry, it’s not too busy today." Pedro sat on a bench and waited. While he waited, he remembered he hadn’t brought the right documents. "What a pain!" he muttered. So, he called his mom and asked for help. Twenty minutes later, his mom arrived with the documents. Pedro thanked her a lot and went to the counter. The clerk looked at the papers and smiled. "Everything’s in order." Before leaving, he asked, "Can I come back tomorrow for something else?" In the end, Pedro left happy. He learned that going to City Hall isn’t so hard.

Património em Perigo: Porque Devemos Preservar as Nossas Raízes

B1-B2 (DEPLE/DIPLE)
🐶 Oreo Sir 小 Tips
汪汪！🍪 親愛的港漂學葡小夥伴們，我是你們的Oreo Sir！今天要跟大家分享一個超級實用的小秘訣，讓你的葡語文章瞬間升級！記住，當你想表達「為了讓...」（目的）時，可以用「a fim de que」+ 虛擬式（conjuntivo），例如「a fim de que preservemos」（為了讓我們保護）。這個連接詞超級高級，讓你的文章聽起來像專業作家！🐾 另外，別忘了用「apesar de」、「no entanto」等連接詞來展現你的邏輯思維，讓考官眼睛一亮！加油，你們都是最棒的汪星人學霸！🍪🐶

English

In recent years, there has been heated debate about the fate of historic buildings in our cities. While many argue that modernization is essential for progress, it is crucial that we do not forget the priceless value of architectural heritage. It is not just about old stones or facades worn by time; it is about the collective memory of a people, the identity that defines us as a society. However, real estate pressure and urban speculation have led to the demolition of countless buildings with historical value, so that skyscrapers or shopping centers can be built. But must progress necessarily erase the past? It is important that authorities implement stricter preservation policies, since once lost, heritage cannot be recovered. Consequently, it is urgent to find a balance between urban development and the protection of our cultural legacy. Furthermore, the rehabilitation of old buildings can bring economic and social benefits. Restoration projects, provided they are well-planned, generate employment and attract tourism, revitalizing degraded urban areas. Therefore, preservation is not synonymous with stagnation, but rather an opportunity to grow sustainably. In conclusion, historical and architectural heritage is not a luxury, but a necessity. It is our responsibility to ensure that future generations can know and appreciate the roots that shaped the present. After all, as the saying goes, «those who do not know history are doomed to repeat it».
