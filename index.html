English

Today I went to Dona Rosa’s bakery with my friend Ana. It was Ana’s birthday, so we wanted to buy a cake. The bakery is small but very pretty. It smells of fresh bread and coffee. When we arrived, Dona Rosa was tidying the shelves. She smiled and said, "Good morning, girls! What will you take today?" I answered, "We’d like a chocolate cake, please." Ana chose the small cake with strawberries because she loves fruit. Then, we ordered two custard tarts to eat at home. While Dona Rosa was wrapping the cake, I saw a little brown dog under the table. Before leaving, Dona Rosa gave us a packet of cookies. I’ll go back to Dona Rosa’s bakery next Saturday.

English

In recent years, remote work has become a reality for millions of people worldwide. Despite offering unprecedented flexibility, this modality is not without its challenges. It is, therefore, essential to analyze its advantages and disadvantages so that everyone can decide whether this is, in fact, the best option for them. On the one hand, working from home allows for greater autonomy. Not only can we manage our time more efficiently, but we also avoid the stress of public transport or chaotic traffic. However, this freedom comes at a price: the line between professional and personal life can become blurred. Furthermore, although technology facilitates communication, collaborating remotely is not always easy. On the other hand, remote work can be a blessing for those living in rural or remote areas. As long as there is a good internet connection, it is possible to access opportunities that would otherwise be out of reach. However, it is important not to underestimate the impact of isolation. The lack of human contact can affect our emotional well-being; therefore, it is crucial to create routines that include moments of socialization, even if virtual. In short, remote work is not a one-size-fits-all solution. While it brings numerous advantages, such as cost reduction and the ability to better balance professional and family life, it requires discipline and organization. It is up to each individual to weigh whether they are ready to embrace this new way of working or if, on the contrary, they prefer the traditional office environment.
