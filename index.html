Yesterday, Ana decided to go to the hairdresser. She hadn’t gone in six months, and her hair was very long. Before leaving, she looked in the mirror and thought: "I need a modern haircut!" When she arrived at the salon, the hairdresser, Rita, smiled and said: "Hello, Ana! What are we doing today?" Ana replied: "I want a short haircut, but not too short. I also want to dye my hair light brown." Rita washed Ana’s hair with a fragrant shampoo and then started cutting. While she cut, they chatted about the weather and holidays. Ana mentioned she was going to travel to the Algarve next month. Rita said: "How nice! The Algarve is beautiful in the summer." After an hour, the haircut was ready. Ana looked in the mirror and was very happy. She paid Rita and left a tip because the work was great. When she got home, her mother said: "Ana, you look beautiful! The new haircut suits you very well." Ana smiled and thought: "Next time, I’ll go earlier!"

English

Living in the city or the countryside is a choice that defines not only our daily lives but also how we view life itself. Although cities offer job opportunities, culture, and leisure that the countryside can hardly match, many people dream of the tranquility and simplicity of rural life. However, is this peace as perfect as it seems? Those who live in the city know that the fast pace can be exhausting. Nevertheless, the diversity of services, from hospitals to restaurants, makes life more convenient. Moreover, the chance to attend theaters, museums, or concerts is something that enriches the soul. Consequently, many city dwellers wouldn’t trade the hustle and bustle of the metropolis for anything, even if they sometimes miss a bit of silence. In the countryside, contact with nature is immediate and invigorating. Since the air is cleaner and the environment less polluted, it’s natural for people to feel healthier. However, in order for this quality of life to be maintained, one must accept that some urban comforts don’t exist. For example, going to the supermarket might mean a half-hour trip, and it’s unlikely there’s a 24-hour pharmacy. Despite everything, those who choose the countryside often do so believing that happiness isn’t measured by the number of shops or cinemas on their doorstep. Therefore, the question isn’t which of the two lifestyles is better, but rather which one suits each of us best. Whatever the choice, what matters is that we feel fulfilled and at peace with our daily lives. After all, what truly counts is that our hearts beat to the right rhythm, whether we’re surrounded by buildings or trees.
