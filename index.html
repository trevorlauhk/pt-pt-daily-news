English

This morning, Rita went to the city library. She loves reading, but she doesn’t have many books at home. Before entering, she saw a poster: "Children’s Book Week – come borrow books!". So, she decided to take the opportunity. Inside the library, Rita looked for the fiction section. She loves romance and adventure stories. After choosing three books, she went to the counter. The librarian, a kind lady, smiled and said: "Are you taking these? They’re very good!". Rita replied: "Yes, please. Can I keep them for two weeks?". The librarian stamped the books and explained: "You can renew them online if you need more time.". Rita thanked her and put the books in her backpack. Before leaving, she remembered she also wanted a cookbook. So, she went back to the shelf and picked one with delicious cakes. On her way home, Rita thought: "I’ll read everything before the end of the month!". She was very happy because now she had new books to enjoy.

Redes Sociais: Aliadas ou Vilãs do Desenvolvimento Juvenil?

B1-B2 (DEPLE/DIPLE)
🐶 Oreo Sir 小 Tips
汪汪！我是Oreo Sir，你的葡語學習好夥伴！🍪 今天來談談這篇文章裡的「conjuntivo」和連接詞，讓你的寫作像小狗追尾巴一樣靈活自如！🐶

注意這些亮點：
1. 「É essencial que os pais *orientem*...」— 這裡的「orientem」就是conjuntivo，因為「é essencial que」表達的是「應該」的概念，不是事實。
2. 「A fim de que este potencial *seja* aproveitado...」— 同樣是conjuntivo，因為「a fim de que」表示目的，後面要用虛擬語氣。
3. 連接詞如「contudo」、「no entanto」、「desde que」等，讓文章邏輯清晰，像我啃骨頭一樣有條不紊！

記住：寫作時多用這些「高級調味料」，你的文章會更有深度，讓考官眼睛一亮！加油，未來的葡語大師！🍪🐾

English

In recent years, social media has become a constant presence in young people’s lives. While it offers countless opportunities for connection and learning, many experts question whether its impact is truly positive. It’s undeniable that platforms like Instagram or TikTok allow teenagers to share their passions and discover new cultures. However, recent studies suggest that excessive use can affect mental health, as constant comparison with seemingly perfect lives generates anxiety and low self-esteem. Nevertheless, we cannot ignore that social media also plays a crucial role in education. Many young people use it to access educational content or even participate in debates on social issues, which consequently contributes to their critical development. In order for this potential to be harnessed, it is essential that parents and educators guide young people to use these tools in a balanced way. Although some argue that social media isolates young people, the truth is that it can also be a space of support. Online communities help many teenagers find like-minded individuals, which is especially important for those who feel marginalized. Therefore, the problem does not lie in social media itself, but in how it is used. As long as there is moderation and awareness, it can be a valuable ally in personal and academic growth.
