Maria is at home on a Saturday night. She is watching television in the living room and eating hot popcorn. Suddenly, she hears a strange noise in the street. She gets up quickly and goes to the window. Outside, it was dark and silent, but she sees a man with a big flashlight. The man was trying to open the door of the neighbour's new car. Maria gets very scared because she is alone at home. She grabs her mobile phone quickly and dials the emergency number 112. She speaks to the operator: "There is a thief in my street, at Rua das Flores, number twelve! I need help quickly!" The operator asks if the man has weapons. He replies: "The police are going to arrive soon." Ten minutes later, the police arrive with their sirens on. The man sees the police cars and runs away into the dark garden. The police officers ask Maria what happened. She closes the window and goes to sleep peacefully.

English

Some say that social media are merely communication tools, but I believe they represent something much deeper in the lives of young people today. Although many parents still do not fully understand this phenomenon, it is undeniable that digital platforms shape behaviors and values. Despite there being undeniable benefits, such as quick access to information, the risks should not be ignored. Many young people spend hours on end in front of screens, which can lead to anxiety problems. It is essential that they learn to use these tools in a balanced way, so that they do not get trapped in a virtual bubble that distorts reality. Since social pressure is constant, it becomes difficult for teenagers to resist comparing themselves. They believe they must achieve unrealistic standards, which can harm their mental health. Therefore, it is essential that families promote digital literacy from an early age. However, we cannot blame technology exclusively. Young people need to develop critical thinking so that they can distinguish between the real and the false. Nevertheless, as long as there is adequate supervision, parents can help their children. In order for the next generation to grow with self-confidence, we need to reflect on our usage. Consequently, media education must be an absolute priority.
