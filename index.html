English

Yesterday, Ana and Pedro went to the zoo with their parents. They left home early because they wanted to see all the animals. When they arrived, there were lots of people, but they didn’t mind. First, they saw the lions. The lions were sleeping under a tree. Then, they went to see the monkeys. The monkeys were jumping from side to side and making lots of silly faces. Ana laughed a lot! Later, they had lunch at the picnic area. They ate sandwiches and drank juice. Pedro also had an ice cream, but he got his T-shirt dirty. In the afternoon, they visited the reptile house. At the end, they saw the penguins. It was a very fun day!

English

In recent years, Portugal has stood out as one of Europe’s most committed countries to the energy transition. Despite facing economic and logistical challenges, the potential of renewable energy in our territory is undeniable. The sun, wind, and water are not only abundant resources but also offer a unique opportunity to reduce our dependence on fossil fuels. However, for this vision to become a reality, it is essential that the government and businesses invest in modern infrastructure and scientific research. One of Portugal’s greatest advantages is its geographical location. With over 300 sunny days a year in the Algarve and strong winds along the northern coast, we have ideal conditions for solar and wind energy production. Nevertheless, there are still obstacles to overcome. For instance, the electrical grid needs modernization so that the energy produced can be distributed efficiently. Furthermore, greater public awareness about adopting more sustainable habits is necessary. Although some critics argue that renewable energies are unstable, technological advances have proven otherwise. Today, energy storage solutions exist that allow us to overcome production fluctuations. Therefore, if we continue on this path, Portugal could not only ensure its energy independence but also export knowledge and technology to other countries. The future is green, and it is up to us to make sure it shines brightly.
