English

João woke up early on Saturday. He wanted to go to Lisbon to visit his grandmother. Before leaving, he had breakfast and drank a coffee. Then, he took his wallet and went to the train station. At the station, there were many people. João got a little nervous. He looked for the ticket office but didn’t find it right away. So, he asked a lady: "Excuse me, where can I buy tickets?" The lady smiled and said: "It’s over there, next to the coffee machine." João thanked her and went to buy the ticket. He told the clerk: "I’d like a ticket to Lisbon, please." The clerk asked: "Round trip or one way?" João thought for a moment and said: "One way, please." The train was leaving in five minutes! He ran to the platform, but the train had already left. João sighed and sat on a bench.

English

There are few moments in life as frustrating as realizing we’ve been misled by a telecommunications contract. Although most companies promise irresistible offers, the reality is that customers often end up paying more than they expected. However, we shouldn’t accept this situation without a fight. After all, complaining is a right we all have, as long as we do it in an informed and persistent way. Firstly, it’s essential that we read all the contract clauses carefully before signing. Nevertheless, even if we’ve done so, unpleasant surprises may arise, such as hidden fees or unsolicited services. In these cases, the first step is to contact the company and demand a clear explanation. It’s important that we stay calm, since an aggressive tone can hinder problem resolution. Therefore, we should record all communications so that we have proof if we need to turn to regulatory bodies. If the company doesn’t resolve the issue, we can file a formal complaint with ANACOM or other competent authorities. Although this process may seem time-consuming, we must not give up, because only then will we ensure our rights are respected. Let’s remember that, even though complaining may be inconvenient, it’s a crucial step to prevent others from going through the same.
