English

Yesterday, Sofia decided to send a package to her grandfather. He lives in the Algarve and has a birthday next week. Sofia bought a book about birds and a warm scarf because her grandfather loves watching birds in the garden. In the morning, she went to the post office. The line was long, but Sofia didn’t mind. After twenty minutes, it was her turn. The clerk asked, "Where is the package going?" and Sofia answered, "To Olhão, please." The clerk weighed the package and said, "That will be five euros and fifty cents." Sofia paid and received the receipt. Before leaving, the clerk explained, "The package will arrive in two or three days." Sofia was happy. She will call her grandfather tomorrow to tell him he has a surprise. Her grandfather will love the gift because he enjoys reading and staying warm in the winter. Then, Sofia will visit her grandfather on the weekend, and they will have tea together.

English

In recent years, social media has become an indispensable part of young people's lives. While it offers numerous advantages, such as the ability to stay in touch with distant friends or access real-time information, many experts question whether these benefits might hide worrying consequences. Since teenagers spend hours scrolling through idealized profiles, it's natural that they develop a distorted view of reality. Consequently, the pressure to be perfect grows, which can lead to self-esteem issues and anxiety. However, we cannot ignore that social media also plays a positive role. So that young people feel more connected, platforms like Instagram or TikTok allow them to share their passions and find communities with similar interests. As long as they are used in moderation, these tools can even stimulate creativity and critical thinking. Nevertheless, it is crucial that parents and educators stay alert, so that the balance between the virtual and real worlds is not lost. While some argue that social media is merely a reflection of society, others believe it amplifies its worst aspects. What is certain is that, whether we like it or not, this influence is here to stay. Therefore, it is up to each of us to decide whether we want social media to be a mirror that inspires us or a prison that limits us.
