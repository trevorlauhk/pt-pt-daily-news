English

Grandma Maria woke up with a headache. She went to the kitchen and drank a glass of water, but the pain didn’t go away. So, she decided to go to the pharmacy near her house. When she arrived, she saw a small line. She waited five minutes and then spoke to the pharmacist: — Good morning! I need medicine for a headache. The pharmacist smiled and showed her three options. Grandma Maria looked at the boxes and chose one. She paid and thanked her. Before leaving, the pharmacist said: — If the pain doesn’t go away in two hours, you should see a doctor. On the way, she also bought a pack of candies for her grandson. Then, she got home, took the medicine, and lay down on the couch. Half an hour later, her headache was already better.

English

In recent years, there has been much talk about sustainability and climate change, however, many people still believe that their individual actions won’t make a difference. Yet, it’s crucial that we understand that every small action counts, since the collective impact can be enormous. Although governments and large corporations bear greater responsibility, this doesn’t mean that we, as ordinary citizens, cannot contribute to a greener future. Therefore, it’s important to adopt sustainable habits in our daily lives so that future generations can enjoy a healthy planet. Reducing plastic consumption, opting for public or shared transport, and saving energy at home are simple measures, but that, if adopted by everyone, can significantly reduce our ecological footprint. Furthermore, it’s essential that we support brands and products that respect the environment, even if this means paying a little more. After all, the cost of not acting will undoubtedly be much higher. It’s true that changing habits isn’t easy, especially when we’re used to a certain lifestyle. However, if each of us makes an effort, no matter how small, we will be building a better world. So, the next time you think about throwing away something that can still be reused, remember: the planet thanks you, and so does the future.
