English

Yesterday morning I went to the bank to open a new account because I need to receive my salary there next month. I arrived at ten o'clock and waited ten minutes in the queue. There were many people there because it is a popular bank. The clerk was very kind and said to me: "Good morning! You need your citizen card and a proof of address." I brought the two documents in my black bag. She looked at the papers for five minutes and then said: "Alright, I am going to open your account now." "It will be a current account with a debit card." It was very quick and easy. Afterwards she gave me a new card and explained to me how to use the app on the mobile phone. I asked: "Can I withdraw money today at the ATM?" She answered: "Yes, you can. The card already works today." I said: "Thank you very much!" She smiled and said: "You're welcome! See you!" Now I have a new account at this bank and I am going to use the card to buy food at the supermarket and to pay the household bills every month. I am also going to pay the school fee with the card. I am very happy because before I didn't have an account here and it was difficult to receive money from my parents. Tomorrow I am going to withdraw some money at the ATM for breakfast and for the bus.

English

Some say the future of the Earth is at risk, but the truth is we still have time to change the course of this story, provided we act urgently. Nowadays, climate change is no longer just a distant scientific forecast, but a reality affecting millions of people worldwide. Many citizens believe that small individual actions make no difference; however, when millions of people decide to change their daily habits, the impact becomes truly significant. For example, although using public transport requires some initial effort and planning, in the long run it considerably reduces each of our carbon footprint. What is essential is that governments implement strict environmental policies so that large companies are forced to drastically reduce their polluting emissions. Until this happens in practice, it is up to each of us to do what is within our reach: recycle properly, save energy at home and consciously choose environmentally friendly products. It is important that we do not lose hope, because, despite the problem seeming overwhelming and complex, there are already innovative technologies that allow us to produce clean energy efficiently. Therefore, if we seriously invest in them and educate new generations about the importance of sustainability, we may perhaps reverse some of the most serious damage caused to our planet. However, it is not enough to just talk about the subject; we need to act concretely and immediately. The planet does not wait for us, so before it is too late, we must make a serious commitment to protect the only home we know and share.
