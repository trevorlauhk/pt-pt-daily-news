English

Yesterday was Ana’s birthday. She turned 25 and invited all her friends to a party at her house. I went with João, my best friend. Before going, we bought a nice gift: a book and a box of chocolates. When we arrived, Ana was very happy. She was wearing a red dress and had a crown on her head. The house was decorated with balloons and colorful lights. First, we ate a delicious cake. Then, we danced and sang happy birthday. João told a funny joke, and everyone laughed. I danced a lot too, but I got tired after an hour. At the end of the party, Ana thanked everyone. She said it was the best birthday of her life. I enjoyed it a lot too. We’ll remember this day forever!

English

Some say that city life is synonymous with opportunities, excitement, and modernity, while others argue that the countryside offers tranquility, fresh air, and a more authentic connection to nature. However, the choice between these two lifestyles is not so straightforward, since both present advantages and disadvantages that go far beyond stereotypes. Living in the city, for example, means having access to healthcare, education, and culture just minutes away. Moreover, the diversity of people and experiences can enrich our worldview. Nevertheless, the fast pace, constant noise, and pollution are factors that, in the long run, can affect well-being. It’s important that each person assesses whether they are willing to sacrifice calm for convenience. On the other hand, the countryside provides an environment where time seems to flow more slowly and naturally. Human relationships tend to be closer, and the simplicity of daily life can bring a sense of peace hard to find in big cities. Despite this, the lack of infrastructure and professional opportunities can be an obstacle, especially for those with ambitions that require a more dynamic setting. Therefore, the decision between city and countryside should not be made based solely on superficial preferences, but by reflecting on what we truly value. Whichever the choice, the key is that it allows us to live fully and happily, as long as we are aware of the challenges each option entails. After all, what matters is not where we live, but how we live.
