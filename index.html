Yesterday, I went to the City Hall with my mom. We needed to renew her citizen card. We left home early because the bus takes a long time. When we arrived, there were many people in line. I got a little nervous, but my mom said, "Don’t worry, everything will be fine." After waiting half an hour, it was our turn. The clerk was very friendly. She asked for the documents and said, "It will take about fifteen minutes." So, my mom and I went to have a coffee. My mom also bought me a pastel de nata. I loved it! When we returned, the card was already ready. The clerk explained everything calmly, and we thanked her. It was a tiring day, but everything went well. Next week, I’ll help my dad go there too.
