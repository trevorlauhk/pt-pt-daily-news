She was nice and asked for my full name, my address and my phone number. I showed my citizen card and my passport. She made a copy of the documents and then we signed some papers together. She said that the account is already active today. Tomorrow I am going to return to the bank to deposit my first money and ask for a new debit card because my old one is very worn out. I am also going to ask how the mobile app works. I am very happy because now I can make online bank transfers without problems and without paying fees. The bank is near my house, on the main street, so it is easy to go there when I need help or when I have questions. Before I did not have an account at this bank, but now everything is simpler.

Entre Culturas: O Desafio de Fazer de Portugal um Lar para Todos

B1-B2 (DEPLE/DIPLE)
🐶 Oreo Sir 小 Tips
🍪汪汪！🐾 Oreo Sir 叮嚀：看到「desde que」、「a fim de que」後面緊接 Conjuntivo（虛擬式），如 saibamos、fique，就像毛孩見到主人回家般搖尾巴興奮！記住：目的句用 subjuntivo，汪！🍪

English

Some say that Portugal is a country of emigrants, but the truth is that, in recent years, it has also become a destination for immigrants. This reality, far from being a threat, represents a unique opportunity to enrich our society, provided that we know how to welcome those who arrive with dignity and respect. Although many immigrants face enormous difficulties in integrating, their contribution to the Portuguese economy and culture is undeniable. However, integration does not happen just because people share the same space; it is necessary that they feel they are part of the community and that their voices are heard. For this process to be successful, it is essential that the State invests in effective reception policies, so that no one is left on the margins of society. Furthermore, it is important to understand the other, despite the cultural differences that may exist. Since we live in a democracy, we have a duty to ensure the same rights for everyone. Therefore, instead of fearing diversity, we should celebrate it. If we manage to create a society where everyone feels at home, we will not only be honouring our values, but also building a stronger Portugal.
