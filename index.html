English

Who hasn’t ever felt trapped by a telecommunications contract that, despite promising the world, ends up being a real headache? It’s common for consumers, once they sign an agreement, to feel powerless in the face of abusive clauses or services that don’t match what was promised. However, complaining shouldn’t be seen as an act of rebellion, but rather as a civic duty, so that companies are held accountable and improve their services. Nevertheless, many hesitate to file complaints, fearing it might complicate things even further. It’s important to remember that, as long as the consumer is well-informed about their rights, there’s no reason to be afraid. For example, if a customer discovers that their contracted package includes hidden fees, they have every right to demand that those charges be waived. Moreover, regulatory bodies like ANACOM are prepared to mediate conflicts, ensuring that both parties reach a fair agreement. Although the process may seem slow, it’s crucial that consumers don’t give up. After all, if no one complains, companies will continue to act as if everything is fine. Therefore, before signing any contract, read it carefully, and if something doesn’t seem clear, question it. And if, even so, you feel wronged, don’t hesitate: complain. Only in this way can we ensure that our rights are respected and that the market becomes more transparent and fair for everyone.
