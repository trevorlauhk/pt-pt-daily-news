English

Last night, Pedro and Ana went out to dinner at a new restaurant. They were very hungry because they hadn’t eaten anything since lunch. When they arrived, a friendly waiter said, "Good evening! A table for two?" Pedro answered, "Yes, please." Then they sat down and opened the menu. Ana looked at the menu and said, "Everything looks delicious! What are you going to order?" Pedro thought for a moment and replied, "I’m going to order a steak with fries. And you?" "I want a chicken salad and vegetable soup," said Ana. The waiter returned and asked, "Do you know what you’re going to order?" "Yes, I’d like a steak with fries, please," said Pedro. The waiter wrote everything down and asked, "Will your drinks be water, or something else?" "Water for me, please," said Pedro. "I’d like an orange juice," said Ana. After some time, the waiter brought the food. Everything was very good! At the end, they asked for the bill and left a tip. They left happy and promised to come back soon.

O 25 de Abril: Uma Revolução que Mudou Portugal para Sempre

English

Almost fifty years ago, Portugal woke up to a new dawn. On April 25, 1974, a group of military officers, tired of the colonial war and the repression of the Salazar regime, decided it was time to act. Although many feared the revolution might fail, the truth is that within a few hours, the country witnessed a profound and peaceful change. The streets filled with red carnations, a symbol of long-awaited freedom, and consequently, the Portuguese people regained fundamental rights that had been denied to them for decades. However, not everything was simple. Since Salazar’s regime had remained in power for nearly half a century, many citizens did not know how to live in a democracy. Nevertheless, the determination of the Portuguese, combined with international support, allowed the country to rebuild itself. It is important, therefore, that we recognize the crucial role this event played in shaping modern Portugal. Without April 25th, we would not have freedom of expression, universal suffrage, or the ability to travel freely. Today, when we look back, we realize that this revolution was not just a historical moment but a milestone that defined the future of generations. So that new generations understand its importance, it is essential that history continues to be taught in schools and celebrated in families. After all, as the saying goes, "Those who do not know history are doomed to repeat it."
