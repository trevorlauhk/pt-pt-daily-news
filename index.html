Yesterday, Pedro's car made a strange noise. It was a loud, metallic sound, like a pot falling. Pedro got worried. He called the garage and scheduled an appointment for the next morning. This morning, Pedro took his car to the garage. The mechanic, Mr. Rui, looked at the engine and fiddled with some parts. Then, he said, "There's a small problem, but it's easy to fix." Pedro felt relieved. He decided to go have a coffee while he waited. At the bakery, he met Maria, a friend from school. Maria smiled and said, "That's great! Afterward, do you want to come with me to the supermarket?" Pedro agreed. He liked helping his friends. When they returned, Mr. Rui had already finished. It was a good day!

English

In recent years, remote work has become an unavoidable reality for millions of people worldwide. While many argue that this model is here to stay, does it bring only advantages, or does it also hide some pitfalls? On one hand, working from home offers unprecedented flexibility. Not only can we manage our time better, but we also avoid the stress of public transport, which often consumes precious hours of our day. Moreover, remote work allows people to live wherever they want, as long as they have a good internet connection. However, not everything is rosy. On the other hand, the lack of in-person contact with colleagues can lead to a sense of isolation. Although some companies try to bridge this gap with virtual meetings, the truth is that human interaction isn’t the same through a screen. It’s important for remote workers to create routines that allow them to socialize outside of work so that they don’t feel lonely. Additionally, the line between personal and professional life tends to blur, which can result in unpaid overtime and, consequently, burnout. In short, remote work has its pros and cons. It’s up to each individual to weigh the advantages and disadvantages, since what works for some may not be ideal for others. Nevertheless, one thing is certain: this new way of working is here to stay, and it’s essential that we adapt to it in the best way possible.
