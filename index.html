João woke up early on Saturday. He wanted to go to Lisbon to visit his grandmother. Before leaving, he ate a piece of toast and drank a coffee. At the station, João saw a long line. He waited for ten minutes, but the line didn’t move. So, he decided to go to the ticket machine. "Good morning! I’d like a ticket to Lisbon, please," he said. João chose the 9:30 train because it was the cheapest. He paid with his card and put the ticket in his pocket. João ran to the platform, but the train had already left. He bought another ticket for the next train at 10:15. This time, he arrived early and sat down to wait. While waiting, he ate a cake and drank some juice. Finally, the train arrived, and João left for Lisbon. His grandmother was very happy when she saw him!

English

In recent years, cryptocurrencies have dominated economic discussions, promising a revolution in the traditional financial system. While many argue that these digital currencies are here to stay, others fear we may be facing a bubble about to burst. But what is the real impact of cryptocurrencies on the global economy? One of the biggest advantages highlighted is decentralization. Unlike conventional currencies, which are controlled by central banks, cryptocurrencies operate through a distributed network, theoretically reducing the influence of governments and institutions. However, this very feature makes them volatile and difficult to regulate. It’s possible that mechanisms ensuring greater stability may emerge in the future, yet for now, the market remains unpredictable. On the other hand, cryptocurrencies have made international transactions easier by eliminating intermediaries and reducing costs. Companies and individuals who rely on remittances or cross-border payments benefit directly from this efficiency. Still, it’s crucial that governments implement clear regulations to prevent fraud and illicit activities. Despite all the excitement, we cannot ignore the risks. Unchecked speculation has already led many investors to lose fortunes, and the lack of legal protection leaves them vulnerable. Therefore, before diving into this world, it’s essential that everyone conducts careful analysis and is aware of the dangers. After all, a financial revolution will only be truly beneficial if it is sustainable and inclusive.
