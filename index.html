English

Rita wants to move house. She’s looking for a small but nice apartment. Yesterday, she saw an ad in the newspaper: "1-bedroom apartment, near the metro, 500 euros per month." So, she called Mr. Silva and scheduled a visit for today at three in the afternoon. She arrived at Rua das Flores at two-thirty. The building was old, but it had a very cute garden. Mr. Silva was already waiting. "Hello, Rita! Please come in," he said with a smile. The apartment had a small living room, a kitchen, a bedroom, and a bathroom. The kitchen was white and clean, but a bit old. The bedroom had a large window and lots of light. "I like this apartment," Rita thought. So, she asked: "Mr. Silva, can you lower the price a little?" Rita was happy. After the visit, Rita went for a coffee. In the end, she smiled. "I think this apartment is good for me!"

However, we must not fall into despair. Each of us has a fundamental role to play, since small daily actions can make a big difference.
