English

In recent years, social media has become an indispensable part of young people's lives. While it offers numerous advantages, such as the ability to stay in touch with distant friends or access real-time information, many experts question whether its impact is truly positive. Since teenagers spend hours browsing platforms like Instagram or TikTok, concerns about their mental and emotional well-being naturally arise. On one hand, social media allows young people to express themselves and find communities with similar interests. However, the pressure to present a perfect life can lead to self-esteem issues. Consequently, it is essential that parents and educators pay attention to signs of anxiety or isolation. Additionally, cyberbullying, which often goes unnoticed, is a reality that cannot be ignored. Although platforms implement measures to combat it, it is crucial that users know how to protect themselves. Nevertheless, we should not demonize social media. As long as it is used consciously, it can be a powerful tool for learning and creativity. In order for young people to make the most of these platforms, it is important that they develop critical thinking. After all, the problem is not the technology itself, but how we use it. Therefore, it is up to all of us — families, schools, and society — to ensure that social media is a safe and enriching space for new generations.
