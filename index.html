English

In a world increasingly dominated by technology and haste, it's easy to forget the value of art and culture. However, despite many considering these areas secondary, the truth is that they play a fundamental role in building a more humane and cohesive society. It's not just about appreciating a painting or watching a play; art is, above all, a mirror of our emotions, our conflicts, and our hopes. Imagine, for example, a city without museums, without concerts, without libraries. It would be a cold place where people would live in isolation, since culture is what allows us to share experiences and create bonds. It is through art that we question the world, put ourselves in others' shoes, and consequently develop empathy. Even if some argue that resources should be invested in "more practical" areas like health or education, it's important to remember that culture is not an accessory but an essential tool for collective well-being. Moreover, art has the power to inspire change. Since humans have existed, cultural expressions have been used to challenge injustices, celebrate diversity, and preserve a people's memory. In order for a society to progress, there must be space for creativity and reflection. Therefore, instead of seeing culture as an expense, we should view it as an investment in the future. After all, a society without art is like a body without a soul: functional, but empty.
