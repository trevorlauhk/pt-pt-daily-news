English

João woke up early on Monday and felt unwell. He had a headache and a fever. He thought, "This isn’t normal." So, he called the doctor’s office and scheduled an appointment for ten o’clock. Before leaving home, João drank a glass of water and put on a jacket because it was cold. Then, he took bus number 7 and arrived at the health center at nine-thirty. He waited in the waiting room with other people. He read an old magazine and drank some hot tea. When it was his turn, the nurse called his name. The doctor examined him carefully and asked, "What’s wrong, João?" He replied, "I have a headache, and I’ve had a fever for two days." The doctor said, "I’ll prescribe some pills. You need to take one in the morning and another at night." João thanked him and went to the pharmacy to buy the medicine. Then, he went back home and lay down. The next day, he already felt better. "How good it is to see a doctor on time!" he thought.

However, we cannot place all the responsibility on the shoulders of world leaders. Each of us has a role to play, as long as we are willing to change some habits. Furthermore, it is crucial that companies adopt more sustainable practices so that excessive consumption can be reduced. Nevertheless, some argue that individual changes will not make a difference, since large industries are the main culprits of pollution. While this perspective has some merit, we cannot ignore that consumer pressure can indeed influence corporate decisions. Therefore, it is essential that we continue to demand eco-friendly products and support brands that care about the environment. Despite the future seeming bleak, there is still hope. If we act now, we may be able to avoid even greater disasters. The planet is calling for help, and it is up to each of us to answer that call. After all, we did not inherit the Earth from our ancestors; we are merely borrowing it from our children.
