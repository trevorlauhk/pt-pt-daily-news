The secretary answered the phone and said: "Good morning! Can you book for tomorrow at 10am?" I replied: "Yes, please! Thank you!" Before leaving home, I took a pill for the pain and washed my face. On the way to the office, it rained a little, but I took the umbrella. I arrived ten minutes before the scheduled time. In the waiting room, I read a magazine and waited ten minutes. Then the doctor called my name. In the office, the doctor examined my tooth and saw a small cavity. She cleaned the tooth and did a simple and quick treatment. After the treatment, I no longer felt any pain. I was very happy because the doctor was very kind and attentive. I am also going to book the next appointment for six months from now.
