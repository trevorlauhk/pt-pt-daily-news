English

Today is a very special day for Ana. She’s going to the library for the first time by herself. Before, her mother always went with her, but today Ana is twelve years old and wants to be independent. When she arrives at the library, Ana sees many books on the shelves. She loves reading, but she doesn’t know which book to choose. So, she decides to ask the librarian for help. The lady is very kind and smiles. — Good morning! Can I help you? — asks the librarian. The librarian takes Ana to a shelf with many colorful books. She shows her three books: one about pirates, another about dogs, and one about a girl who travels the world. Ana chooses the book about pirates and the book about dogs. Then, the librarian teaches her how to fill out the borrowing form. When she leaves the library, Ana feels very proud. Now, she has two new books to read and a new adventure ahead of her!

Cidades que Andam: O Futuro da Mobilidade Urbana Depende de Nós

B1-B2 (DEPLE/DIPLE)
🐶 Oreo Sir 小 Tips
汪汪！我是Oreo Sir，你的葡語學習好夥伴🍪！今天來聊聊「連接詞」這個大餅乾🍪！你看到「a fim de que」、「desde que」這些詞了嗎？它們都是「虛擬式（conjuntivo）」的好朋友！記住：當你表達願望、目的或條件時，就要請出虛擬式這位大明星！比如「É necessário que haja...」（必須有...），「haja」就是虛擬式哦！別怕用錯，多練習就會像我一樣自信地搖尾巴啦！加油，未來的葡語達人！🐾🍪

English

In recent years, Portuguese cities have witnessed a true revolution in how we get around. Although public transport has improved significantly, many citizens still prefer private cars, since they offer greater flexibility. However, this choice has serious consequences: endless traffic jams, air pollution, and an increasingly sedentary lifestyle. Therefore, it is urgent to rethink urban mobility so that our cities become more sustainable and healthy. It is undeniable that buses, metros, and trains are viable alternatives, provided they are efficient and accessible. Nevertheless, for people to choose these options, there must be a well-planned and frequent network. Furthermore, bike lanes and pedestrian paths should be expanded, even though many local authorities fear reducing space for cars. If this happens, we can not only reduce our ecological footprint but also promote a more active life. Of course, changing habits is not easy. Many argue that public transport does not reach all areas or that it is unreliable. However, these criticisms only reinforce the need to invest more in the sector. After all, a city that moves is a city that breathes—and we all deserve to live in an environment where it is possible to get around without stress, pollution, or with a better quality of life. The future of urban mobility is in our hands. Shall we take the first step?
