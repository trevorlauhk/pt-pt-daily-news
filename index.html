Yesterday, Ana and Pedro went to the Lisbon Zoo. They woke up early because they wanted to see all the animals. First, they saw the lions. The lions were sleeping under a tree. Then, they went to see the monkeys. The monkeys were jumping from side to side and making noise. Ana laughed a lot! Later, they had lunch at the zoo restaurant. They ate sandwiches and drank juice. Pedro also ate an ice cream, but he dropped some on the ground. A bird came right away and ate it all! In the afternoon, they saw the dolphins. Ana said, "They are beautiful!" It was a very fun day. They will return next summer!

Despite facing economic and logistical challenges, the potential of renewable energy in our territory is undeniable. The sun, which so often warms and illuminates us, could indeed be our greatest asset. However, for this to happen, it is essential that we invest not only in technology but also in policies that encourage the population to adopt these solutions. One of Portugal’s greatest advantages is its geographical location. With over 2,500 hours of sunshine per year, it is natural that solar energy should be an obvious choice. Nevertheless, we cannot overlook other sources, such as wind or hydroelectric power, which have already proven efficient. Therefore, a diversified strategy will be crucial to ensure the stability of energy supply. Many families still hesitate to install solar panels, since the initial investment can be high. In order for everyone to benefit from this green revolution, the government should create more financial support programs. Furthermore, it is vital that companies invest in innovation so that costs decrease and the technology becomes more accessible. If we continue on this path, Portugal could not only reduce its dependence on fossil fuels but also become an example for other countries. After all, the future of renewable energy does not depend solely on natural resources but on the collective will to build a more sustainable planet. May our brightness come not only from the sun but also from our determination.
