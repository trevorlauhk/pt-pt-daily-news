English

Yesterday, Ana went to the City Hall because she needed a new document. She woke up early and had a quick breakfast. Then, she put on her nicest clothes and left home at eight-thirty. When she arrived at the City Hall, she saw a long line. "Oh no!" she thought. But it wasn’t a problem because she had brought a book to read. While she waited, she read ten pages and chatted with a friendly lady. Finally, it was her turn. The clerk was very kind and explained everything calmly. Ana handed over her papers and paid a small fee. Then, the clerk said, "Come back in three days to pick up your document." Ana left happy. On her way home, she met her friend Rita and told her the story. Rita laughed and said, "I’ll need to go there soon too!" Ana thought it was a great idea, and they agreed to go together next time.

English

In recent years, remote work has become an unavoidable reality for many professionals. While it offers unprecedented flexibility, this mode of work raises questions that cannot be ignored. Are we truly prepared to give up the traditional office? One of the biggest advantages of remote work is the ability to manage time more autonomously. However, this freedom requires iron discipline, since distractions at home are plentiful. It is essential that everyone establishes clear routines so that productivity is not compromised. Furthermore, the reduction in commuting time allows workers to invest more time in themselves or their families. Yet, not everything is rosy. Loneliness and isolation can become serious issues, especially for those who value human contact. Although video calls help bridge this gap, it is undeniable that nothing replaces an in-person conversation. On the other hand, the difficulty in separating workspace from personal space can lead to a feeling that one never truly «switches off» from work. Therefore, before choosing this mode of work, it is crucial to weigh both the benefits and the challenges. Remote work can be a blessing for some but a trap for others. It all depends on how each person approaches it and, above all, how they organize themselves so that this new way of working is sustainable in the long run.
