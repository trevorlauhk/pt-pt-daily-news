Today is the big day! Rita is traveling alone for the first time. She’s a little nervous, but also very excited. The plane leaves at three in the afternoon, so she arrives at the airport two hours early. At the airport, Rita looks for the airline counter. There are many people, but she sees the sign with her airline’s name. "Good morning! Can I see your ticket and passport, please?" Rita hands over the documents, and the attendant checks everything. Then, she weighs the suitcase. "Your suitcase weighs 18 kilos. It’s within the limit." Rita feels relieved because she doesn’t have to pay extra. The attendant gives Rita the boarding pass and explains the gate and time. Before passing through security, she takes off her jacket and puts her things in the tray. A security guard asks her to open her backpack. "All good," he says. Rita smiles and thinks, "Almost there!"

English

Choosing between living in the city or the countryside defines not only our daily lives but also our relationship with the world. While cities offer professional opportunities and a vibrant cultural life, the countryside provides tranquility and a closer connection to nature. However, the decision isn’t that simple, since each lifestyle has its own advantages and disadvantages. In the city, everything seems within reach: efficient public transport, countless shops and services, and a cultural scene that never lets us get bored. Yet, the fast pace and constant noise can be exhausting. That’s why many people dream of escaping to the countryside, where the air is clean and the days seem longer. Despite the countryside offering a calmer life, not everyone can adapt to the lack of conveniences or the distance from urban centers. Some argue that happiness lies in balance. Therefore, some families choose to live on the outskirts of cities so they can enjoy the best of both worlds. Others, however, believe it’s impossible to reconcile the two realities and prefer to fully embrace one. Either way, it’s important that each person reflects on what they truly value in life. One thing is certain: no matter where we live, what truly matters is that we feel fulfilled. After all, the ideal place is where our heart feels at peace.
