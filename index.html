At the salon, the hairdresser, Rita, greeted her with a smile. "Hello, Ana! What are we doing today?", she asked. Ana replied: "I want a short cut, but not too short." Rita showed her some magazines and said: "Look at these photos." Ana chose a cut she saw on a famous actress. After washing her hair, Rita started cutting. Ana closed her eyes and relaxed. Half an hour later, the cut was ready. Rita showed her the result with a large mirror. Ana was surprised: "I love it! It’s even better than I imagined!" In the end, Ana paid and thanked Rita. When she got home, she showed her new look to her mother.

English

In recent years, the internet has become a space where everyone can share their opinions, which, at first glance, seems like a triumph of freedom of expression. However, this freedom brings with it a complex dilemma: how to distinguish reliable information from disinformation that spreads at an alarming speed? Despite social media allowing us to connect with people worldwide, they have also become fertile ground for fake news, which often has serious real-life consequences. Since disinformation can influence elections, worsen health crises, or even incite hatred, it is essential to have mechanisms to combat it. However, important questions arise: who decides what is true? And, more crucially, how to do so without limiting freedom of expression, a right that was so hard-won? Some argue that digital platforms should take greater responsibility by filtering harmful content, while others believe that any form of censorship is dangerous and could be used to silence legitimate voices. Therefore, a delicate balance must be found. Digital literacy, for instance, could be a solution, provided it is implemented comprehensively. After all, if people are better prepared to identify lies, perhaps disinformation will lose its power. Additionally, laws must be clear and transparent so that there are no abuses by governments or companies. The challenge is enormous, but we cannot ignore it, as the future of democracy may depend on the answers we find today.
