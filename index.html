Ana woke up early on Saturday. She had a plan: to buy a new dress for her friend's party. Before leaving, she had breakfast and put on her favorite pants. Then, she walked to the shopping mall. At the store, Ana saw many beautiful dresses. A friendly saleswoman approached and asked, "Can I help you?" Ana tried them all, but the red one was too tight, and she didn’t like the black one much. So, she chose the blue dress. It fit perfectly! She paid with the money she had saved and left happily. On her way home, she met her friend Rita. Rita said, "Ana, you look beautiful! The dress is lovely!" Ana smiled and replied, "Thank you! I’ll wear it to your party." At night, Ana showed the dress to her mother. Her mother hugged her and said, "You made a great choice!" Ana went to bed happy, looking forward to the party.

English

In recent years, Portugal has witnessed a significant increase in immigration, a phenomenon that, while bringing numerous economic and cultural benefits, also raises complex questions about integration. It is undeniable that diversity enriches the social fabric, however, for this richness to be truly harnessed, effective policies and a society open to dialogue are essential. Many immigrants arrive in the country with the dream of a better life, yet they do not always find the ideal conditions to integrate. It is crucial that the government invests in language learning programs, since mastering Portuguese is the key to accessing qualified jobs and actively participating in the community. Furthermore, companies should be encouraged to hire people from different backgrounds so that multiculturalism is reflected not only on the streets but also in the workplace. Despite some persistent challenges, such as prejudice or bureaucracy, there are encouraging signs that Portuguese society is evolving. Community projects, cultural festivals, and volunteer initiatives show that when there is willingness, it is possible to build harmonious coexistence. Therefore, it is up to all of us—citizens, institutions, and governments—to work together so that Portugal continues to be an example of hospitality and mutual respect.
