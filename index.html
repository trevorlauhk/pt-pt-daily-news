English

Maria entered a new restaurant in the centre of Lisbon. The space was small but very cosy and smelled good of fresh bread. There were many people inside, but she quickly found a table near the window. She sat down because she was very hungry today. The waiter arrived with a friendly smile and gave her the big, colourful menu. She read the options very carefully for a few minutes because she wanted to choose well. That day she wanted to eat fresh fish of the day and chose the cod with potatoes, green salad and olive oil. The waiter wrote the order in a small notebook and asked if she wanted something to drink at that moment. Maria said she wanted a still water and a glass of red wine, please. After ten minutes, the food arrived at the table still hot. The cod was very good and the potatoes were also hot and tasty. Maria ate everything slowly and was very satisfied with the meal. At the end, she asked the waiter for the bill and he brought it. She paid by credit card quickly and left the restaurant very happy. She thought she was going to come back next week.

Quatro dias de trabalho: utopia ou o futuro inevitável do emprego?

B1-B2 (DEPLE/DIPLE)
🐶 Oreo Sir 小 Tips
Oreo Sir 汪汪！🍪 記得「embora」和「desde que」後面一定要接 Conjuntivo（例如 possa、reorganizem）！學葡文就像咬 Oreo 餅乾一樣，慢慢嚼先最香甜入心，汪汪為你打氣！🍪

English

For some time now, several European countries have been discussing the possibility of reducing the working week to four days without any pay cut. Although this proposal may seem utopian to many traditional employers, the results of pilot tests recently conducted in Portugal and the United Kingdom suggest that working less does not necessarily mean producing less. In fact, since workers feel more rested and motivated, productivity tends to increase significantly, provided that companies reorganize their internal processes in an efficient and sustainable way. However, not all economic sectors can easily adapt to this new work model. For example, hospitals and emergency services, where it is necessary to guarantee coverage five days a week, face quite complex logistical challenges. Furthermore, it is essential that the monthly salary is not reduced, so that employees maintain their purchasing power. Many experts argue that, if we implement this measure gradually, we can improve quality of life without harming economic growth. However, there needs to be a serious dialogue between unions, government and companies, so that we can find a realistic consensus. Is our society truly prepared to take this step forward? Perhaps it is time to experiment with new models, because, despite there being obstacles, the benefits for mental health are undeniable. Therefore, the four-day week may come to be a transformative reality that changes our society for the better.
