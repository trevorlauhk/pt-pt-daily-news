However, the reality is quite different: glaciers are melting at an alarming rate, temperatures are breaking records year after year, and extreme phenomena like droughts and floods are becoming increasingly frequent. Therefore, it is urgent to act, not only on a global level but also in our daily lives. Since every small action counts, it is essential that we adopt more sustainable habits. Ever since we started recycling, reducing plastic consumption, or choosing public transport, we have already taken important steps. So that future generations can live on a healthy planet, it is necessary that we all do our part. However, it is not enough to just change individual behaviors; governments and companies must also take responsibility. Consequently, it is crucial to pressure them to implement stricter environmental policies. Although the outlook may seem bleak, there is still hope. If each of us commits to making a difference, perhaps we can reverse this trend. After all, as the proverb says, "prevention is better than cure." And you, are you willing to do your part?
