Before I didn't have any insurance for the car, but now I realize it is very important to be protected on the road every day. Tomorrow morning I am going to sign the contract at the branch in the city center. After that, I am going home very happy and secure. I also want to get insurance for my house next week. My neighbor says it is essential to have protection against fires and floods. He has had insurance for three years and is very satisfied with the insurance company. This afternoon I am going to call the insurance company to schedule a quick meeting with them. I am very happy because tomorrow I am going to have my first life insurance and I am going to sleep better at night. My friend is also going to get insurance tomorrow.

A escola que o século XXI exige de nós

English

We live in an era where information is just a click away, so education can no longer be limited to transmitting content that quickly becomes obsolete. It is essential that young people develop critical, creative and socio-emotional skills, since the job market is constantly changing and demands increasingly versatile professionals. Therefore, schools must transform into spaces where one truly learns how to learn. developing intellectual autonomy that allows them to face unforeseen challenges throughout life. However, it is not enough to introduce technology in classrooms if methods remain the same. Teachers need to create environments where students feel motivated to question and collaborate, so that they can adapt to the rapid technological and social changes. Provided that there is continuous investment in teacher training, it will be possible to build relevant education. Despite there being social inequalities that persist, the role of education involves promoting inclusion and equity. However, for this to happen, it is necessary that all of society participates in this process. If we want to prepare citizens for a changing world, we must ensure that everyone has access to quality education. regardless of their socioeconomic background or the context in which they live.
