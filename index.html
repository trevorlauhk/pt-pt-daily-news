English

We live in an era where cities are growing at a dizzying pace, and with them, the challenges of urban mobility. Although many still prefer private cars, it is undeniable that public transport represents the most sustainable and efficient solution to traffic chaos. However, for this change to happen, it is essential that governments invest not only in infrastructure but also in policies that encourage the use of buses, metro, and trains. In Lisbon and Porto, for example, significant improvements have been observed in recent years. Nevertheless, there is still much to be done. Since the population continues to grow, it is urgent to create more lines, extended schedules, and affordable fares so that everyone can benefit from these services. Furthermore, it is crucial that cities are planned with pedestrians and cyclists in mind, as this is the only way to reduce car dependency. Although some argue that public transport is slow or unreliable, the truth is that, with good planning, it can become the backbone of urban mobility. Therefore, it is vital that citizens also do their part by choosing to leave their cars at home whenever possible. After all, cleaner and less congested cities are a benefit for everyone, provided there is a willingness to change. Thus, the future of our cities depends on smart decisions today. If we want to breathe clean air and have safer streets, it is time to rethink how we get around. And you, have you chosen the bus this week?
