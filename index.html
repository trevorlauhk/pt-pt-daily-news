English

Portuguese gastronomy is often celebrated for its simplicity and authenticity; however, in recent years, it has surprised the world with a remarkable evolution. Although many fear that modernization might erase traditional roots, the truth is that Portuguese chefs have managed to innovate without losing the essence of what makes us unique. It is fascinating to observe how century-old dishes, such as *bacalhau à brás* or *cozido à portuguesa*, continue to be revered, while bold creations that challenge the palate emerge at the same time. One of the reasons this evolution has been so successful is the respect for seasonality and local products. Ever since Portugal opened up to the world, foreign ingredients have begun to integrate into our cuisine, but always with a Portuguese touch. For instance, the use of spices like saffron or turmeric, which arrived through trade routes, now breathes new life into classic dishes. However, it is crucial that these influences do not overshadow what is ours, so that the gastronomic identity remains recognizable. Another interesting aspect is how technology has entered kitchens. Today, it is possible to find restaurants that use cutting-edge techniques, such as molecular cuisine, to reinterpret old recipes. Yet, even in these cases, the goal is not to replace but to enrich. After all, who would have thought that a simple *pastel de nata* could be served with a cinnamon foam or that a seafood *cataplana* would gain a deconstructed version? These experiments show that, as long as there is creativity and respect for tradition, Portuguese gastronomy can continue to evolve without losing its soul. Therefore, the next time you taste a Portuguese dish, remember: you are not just savoring food, but a story that renews itself with every bite.
