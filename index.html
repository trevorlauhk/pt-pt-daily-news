English

Mrs. Ana lives alone in a small apartment in Lisbon. She loves her home, but last night she had a problem: the kitchen tap broke, and water started gushing out quickly! She tried to turn it off, but couldn’t. So, she called Mr. João, her neighbor from the first floor. He came quickly and helped her shut off the water. Then, he said: "Mrs. Ana, you need home insurance." The next day, Mrs. Ana went to the insurance company near her home. The employee explained: "We have a good and affordable insurance plan." Mrs. Ana thought for a moment and said: "Yes, I want to get this insurance." Now, Mrs. Ana pays thirty euros a month. She knows that if another problem happens, she won’t spend a lot of money. At night, she sleeps better and even dreams of chocolate cakes...

English

When we think about quality of life, the same question always arises: is it better to live in the hustle and bustle of the city or the tranquility of the countryside? Although both options have undeniable advantages, the choice largely depends on what each person values in their daily life. In the city, everything seems to be just a click or a short metro ride away. There is an endless supply of services, from restaurants offering cuisines from around the world to theaters and museums that allow us to travel without leaving the place. However, this fast pace comes at a cost: stress builds up, the air is often unbreathable, and the constant noise can become exhausting. It’s true that for those who love movement, the city never sleeps, but can we really rest in such an environment? The countryside, on the other hand, offers a completely different scene. Here, time seems to flow more slowly, as if nature dictates the rhythm of our lives. Waking up to the sound of birds instead of cars is an experience many city dwellers don’t know. However, living in the countryside requires being prepared for another reality: services are scarce, public transport is almost nonexistent, and sometimes loneliness can be felt. Still, for those seeking peace and a closer connection to the land, there is no place like it. Therefore, the question is not which of the two is better, but which one better suits what we are looking for. Since each person has different priorities, it is important to reflect on what truly makes us happy. After all, what matters is that, wherever we are, our heart beats in harmony with the place we choose to call home.
