Yesterday, Pedro’s car made a strange noise. It was a loud, metallic sound, as if something was broken. Pedro got worried because the car is very important to him. He uses the car every day to go to work and take his kids to school. So, Pedro decided to take the car to the garage. Before leaving home, he checked the oil and water, but he didn’t find anything wrong. Then, he called the garage and scheduled an appointment for three in the afternoon. When he arrived at the garage, the mechanic looked at the car and said, "Let’s see what’s going on." Pedro waited in the waiting room and had a coffee. After half an hour, the mechanic came to him and explained, "There’s a problem with the belt." Pedro asked, "How long will it take?" The mechanic replied, "It’ll take two hours." Pedro decided to go back home and do other things. He’ll pick up the car tomorrow morning. Now, Pedro is more relaxed.

Contratos de Telecomunicações: Quando Reclamar Não é um Capricho, mas um Direito

English

Who hasn’t ever felt like a hostage to a telecommunications contract that, despite promising the world, ends up becoming an endless headache? It’s common for consumers to hesitate to complain, fearing their voice won’t be heard or, worse, that the situation might worsen. However, it’s essential to remember that once we sign a contract, we have rights that must be respected. Imagine you subscribed to an internet package that, from day one, has constant failures. You contact customer service, but despite assurances that the problem will be fixed, nothing changes. In these situations, many give up, thinking it’s not worth insisting. Yet, it’s precisely in these moments that we should persist, so that the company fulfills its obligations. Complaining isn’t about being difficult—it’s about demanding what we’re owed. Therefore, before passively accepting an unfair situation, educate yourself about your rights as a consumer. Often, providers rely on our passivity to avoid solving problems. So, if the internet keeps failing or the service doesn’t match what was promised, don’t hesitate: complain. Moreover, there are regulatory bodies, like ANACOM, that can step in if the company doesn’t respond appropriately. It’s important for consumers to know they’re not alone and that, as long as they act within the law, they have every right to demand quality. After all, paying for a service that doesn’t work is like throwing money out the window—and no one deserves that.
