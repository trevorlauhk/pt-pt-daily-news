English

Rita loves reading. Every week, she goes to the library near her house. Yesterday, she decided to borrow three new books. When she arrived at the library, Rita greeted the librarian. "Good morning, Mrs. Clara! Today I want to take some books home," she said with a smile. Mrs. Clara replied, "Of course, Rita! Do you have your library card?" Rita searched her backpack and showed the card. Then, she went to the shelves and chose an adventure book. Finally, she picked up a cookbook because she wants to learn to cook better. Mrs. Clara scanned the books on the computer and said, "You can take these books for two weeks." Rita thanked her and promised, "I’ll read everything quickly and bring them back next Monday!" On her way home, Rita was already eager to start reading. She thought, "Tomorrow I’ll read the adventure book before bed."

O Planeta Pede Socorro: Será que Ainda Vamos a Tempo?

English

In recent years, climate change has been a recurring topic in everyday conversations, news reports, and even on social media. Although many governments and organizations are taking steps to combat this issue, the truth is that the effects are already being felt: prolonged droughts, forest fires, and devastating floods have become almost routine. However, we must not forget that each of us plays a fundamental role in this fight, since our daily choices directly influence the planet's future. It is undeniable that sustainability is no longer an option but a necessity. Therefore, it is urgent that we adopt more responsible habits, such as reducing plastic consumption, opting for renewable energy, or even prioritizing public transport. Nevertheless, some argue that these changes are insufficient, given that the biggest polluters remain industries. Still, it is important that we do not give up, for, as the proverb says, "drops of water make the ocean." What is at stake is not just the survival of future generations but also our quality of life in the present. Thus, it is essential that citizens demand more ambitious policies and that governments act with the speed the situation requires. After all, if we do not act now, it may be too late to reverse the damage caused. The question that arises is: are we willing to make the necessary sacrifices so that the planet remains a habitable place?
